Our thesis

Students don't fail because they're lazy. They fail because the material is too wide.

If a lesson can't be traced back to the course outline or a past question, it doesn't belong in SKI.

Lecturers move fast and assume prior knowledge. YouTube gives you the whole internet and no direction. AI gives you confident answers to questions you were never asked. SKI does one thing: it teaches exactly what your exam will ask, and nothing else.

About

Built from what students actually told us.

THE PAIN

Too much material, no priority

Students told us the biggest problems are lecturers jumping ahead, too few examples, and not knowing what to prioritise before an exam.

THE WORKAROUND

Ask AI, re-read notes, search YouTube

That's what happens when you're stuck. Each one fails differently: AI is inaccurate, notes are static, YouTube can't answer a question about the exact part you're watching.
